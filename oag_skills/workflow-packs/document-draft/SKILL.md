# Document Draft Pack

## Procedure
1. Collect: audience, purpose, key points, desired length, tone.
2. Outline first; show the outline for approval.
3. Draft after outline approval. No em dashes. Short sentences.
4. Offer one revision pass focused on cuts, not additions.

## Output contract
- Outline as numbered sections.
- Draft under the agreed word budget.
- A change log for the revision pass.
